My First Game with AI, Built in Three Hours

Fireline is a light, addictive firefighter game. It was my first game made with AI: Claude connected to Higgsfield and other plugins, with the look designed in Figma.

Play Fireline

Problem

Game UI that only lives in Figma never gets tested by a thumb, with a timer running and fire spreading behind it.

Solution

A short, focused game loop: put out every fire, rescue the people inside and don't waste water, in my own Figma design.

Goal

A game people understand in seconds and want to play again.

Role Idea, game design, UI · Tools Figma, Claude, Higgsfield · Platform Browser, phone and desktop

Where the idea came from

I wanted to learn how to build a game with AI, so I connected Claude to Higgsfield and a few more plugins, and designed the look in Figma.

The game itself was my own idea: something light and simple, quick to understand and hard to put down.

Process & Decisions

  • Water as the main resource: every shot is a small decision
  • Two nozzles: a safe wide spray and a strong jet
  • A HUD you can read at a glance, even with fire behind it
  • Stars, coins and upgrades as a reason to replay

How I thought it through

Fireline in 13 seconds

A playable build in about three hours: eight fire calls, five upgrades, a hero screen and a full HUD.

Visual language

Teal spotlight backgrounds, navy panels with a hard ink outline, slanted yellow name plates and Lilita One in white caps.

What I took away

A time box sharpens decisions. Three hours forced one clear core loop.

Game UI works under pressure. Contrast and hard outlines matter even more here.

Playable beats perfect. A short build says more than any static frame.
