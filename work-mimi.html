From Instagram DMs to a Live Store

Mimi Vintage is a vintage boutique in Herzliya where every piece is one of a kind. I took it from selling in Instagram messages to a live Shopify store on its own domain.

Visit the live store

Problem

Selling through DMs meant the same questions every day, items held for people who never paid, and money moved by hand.

Solution

A clean Shopify store where each piece can be found and paid for in a few taps, with Instagram kept for pickup and delivery.

Goal

Let customers buy on their own, any time, and get the store approved for card payments.

Role UX/UI design, Shopify build, launch · Tools Shopify, Figma · Platform Web + mobile, English and Hebrew

Process & Decisions

  • Photos lead: real studio shots show each piece honestly
  • Pay on the site, talk on Instagram for pickup or delivery
  • Categories built for one-offs, with the size in the name
  • "You may also like" in the cart, before checkout

How I thought it through

Built for the phone

Most of Mia's customers come from Instagram, so the store was designed phone first.

And on the desktop

Large photos next to the price and one clear "Add to cart".

Getting approved to take payments

A card company reviews every Israeli store before it can charge cards. The first review found three gaps, and I fixed each where a reviewer looks.

  • Address and phone in the footer, terms and contact page
  • A refund policy written for one-of-a-kind pieces
  • A product liability section for second hand items

The store was approved and now takes card payments on mimivintage.com.

Domain & search

I pointed mimivintage.com to the new store without moving the registration or breaking email, and gave the home page a bilingual Google title.

What I took away

Launching is more than designing. Domains, payments and legal pages decide when the first sale happens.

Keep what already works. Customers love talking to Mia, so the conversation stays on Instagram.

Write for every reader. A payment reviewer reads a site differently from a shopper.
