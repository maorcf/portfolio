Designing Pazi, a Career Guide for Young Israelis

Pazi is a mobile app that helps young adults, especially Israelis just out of the army, find what to study and what to work in, through playful questions and an AI chat.

Problem

Many young Israelis finish their service with no idea what comes next, and career tests feel like exams.

Solution

A gamified diagnostic: short, tappable questions, a friendly mascot and an AI chat that ends with a clear next step.

Goal

Get more people to finish the diagnostic and take a real next step.

Role UX research, UX/UI design · Tools Figma · Platform iOS + Android, Hebrew

User Research

Who Pazi is for, and what holds them back

Just out of the army Helps his family

Roi, 23

Former combat soldier from Netanya. Wants to help his parents and build a stable future.

“All my friends already know what they're doing. I don't even know where to start.”
  • WantsA clear direction that also pays the bills
  • Can't standLong tests that end with no real answer
  • So I designedA short, playful diagnostic that ends with one course to sign up for
Torn between options Overthinks

Noa, 21

Finished national service. Has a notebook full of ideas and can't choose one.

“I like too many things. I need someone to help me narrow it down.”
  • WantsUnderstand what really suits her
  • Can't standGeneric advice that fits everyone
  • So I designedPersonal questions on interests, colors and habits, then a matched result
Wants a change No time for courses

Itay, 28

Has worked in cafés since the army. Wants a real career but works long shifts.

“I don't have time to go to a counselor. I need something I can do on my break.”
  • WantsExplore new careers in short bits of time
  • Can't standForms he has to finish in one sitting
  • So I designedOne question per screen and an AI chat he can pick up anytime

Research & Inspiration

Aptitude tests feel clinical and exhausting. I studied brain-game apps to see how they make testing fun:

  • Games over exams: short puzzles instead of multiple choice
  • A friendly face softens the stakes
  • Instant, colorful feedback keeps people going

Process & Decisions

  • A mascot as a guide at every step
  • One short question per screen with a big "Continue"
  • Icon and color cards instead of multiple choice
  • Results that end in an action, like a course to sign up for

What I took away

The tone of a test matters. The same questions feel different as a game than as an exam.

Design for a real moment. Roi's worry about money and direction kept the flow grounded.

A result should end in an action. The next step has to be obvious.
